Pulse Drop Arcade

Privacy Policy

Effective date: September 7, 2026

Overview

Pulse Drop Arcade is designed to be a simple offline arcade game. The app does not require an account and does not ask for personal information.

Data collection

Pulse Drop Arcade does not collect, transmit, sell, or share personal data. Gameplay information such as high scores, coin balance, settings, and other progress may be stored locally on your device so the game can remember your progress.

Tracking and advertising

The app does not use advertising SDKs and does not track users across apps or websites.

Device permissions

The app does not require access to your location, contacts, photos, camera, microphone, health data, or other sensitive device information for normal gameplay.

Children's privacy

Because the app does not collect personal data, it does not knowingly collect personal information from children.

Changes to this policy

If the app's data practices change in a future version, this policy will be updated accordingly.

Contact

For privacy or support questions, use the Pulse Drop Arcade support page.
