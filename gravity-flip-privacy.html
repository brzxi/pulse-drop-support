GRAVITY FLIP

Privacy Policy

Last updated: September 8, 2026

Overview

GRAVITY FLIP is an iOS arcade game. The game does not require account registration and does not directly collect your name, email address, phone number, contacts, precise location, photos, or other personal content.

Game progress, settings, scores, unlocked items, and other gameplay data are stored locally on your device.

Advertising

GRAVITY FLIP uses Google AdMob to provide optional rewarded advertisements. Players may voluntarily choose to watch a rewarded advertisement in exchange for an in-game benefit, such as continuing a run or receiving additional in-game Energy.

Google Mobile Ads may process certain information related to advertising and app usage, such as device information, IP address, advertising data, diagnostic information, and interactions with advertisements. GRAVITY FLIP does not sell personal information.

Advertising personalization and data processing may depend on your region, device settings, and consent choices.

Consent

Where required by applicable law, users may be presented with a consent message before advertising-related data is processed. Users can make the privacy choices available to them through the consent interface.

App Tracking Transparency

GRAVITY FLIP does not currently request permission to track users across apps and websites using Apple’s App Tracking Transparency framework.

Third-Party Services

The app uses Google AdMob / Google Mobile Ads SDK for optional rewarded advertisements. No third-party account, authentication, payment, or analytics service is required to use the game.

In-App Purchases

GRAVITY FLIP does not currently offer real-money in-app purchases or subscriptions.

Children's Privacy

GRAVITY FLIP is not designed to knowingly collect personal information directly from children. Advertising and privacy requirements may be applied according to applicable laws, platform requirements, and advertising service settings.

Data Security

Core game progress is stored locally on the user's device. Third-party advertising services are responsible for information they process according to their own privacy and security practices.

Changes to This Policy

This Privacy Policy may be updated if the app's features, advertising implementation, or privacy practices change. Any updates will be published on this page.

Contact

For privacy-related questions regarding GRAVITY FLIP, please contact the developer through the support information provided on the app's App Store listing.
